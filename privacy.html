Privacy Policy

How SSA Consulting Inc. handles information submitted through this website.

Last updated: September 26, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SSA Consulting Inc. handles information you submit through this website, including when you contact us about our services.

2. Information we collect

When you voluntarily submit our contact form, we receive the information you provide. This may include:

  • Your full name and email address.
  • Your company or business name, if provided.
  • The services you select as being of interest.
  • Your message and any other information you choose to share.

The form requires your name, email address and message. Company details and services of interest are optional. Please share only the information needed for your inquiry and avoid including sensitive personal information.

3. How we use information

We use information submitted through the website to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and understand the services you need.
  • Communicate with you about your business needs and our services.
  • Maintain business correspondence and relevant records.

4. Contact form processing

Our contact form uses Web3Forms. When you submit the form, the information you enter is transmitted through Web3Forms so your inquiry can be delivered to SSA Consulting Inc.

Web3Forms processes submission information to provide its service, including delivery and spam prevention. For details about its handling of information, see the Web3Forms Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing of information

SSA Consulting Inc. does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the website and handle inquiries, such as Web3Forms. We may also disclose information where required by applicable law.

6. Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use advertising or tracking cookies, and we have not installed analytics tools to track visitor activity.

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts and some script files from external content delivery networks. Your browser contacts those providers to retrieve the files, which may involve technical information such as your IP address and browser details. These requests support the website’s appearance and functionality.

7. Data retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, maintain business records or meet applicable obligations. The appropriate period depends on the nature of the information and the reason it was provided.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information submitted to us. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be absolutely secure.

9. Third-party links

This website may link to external websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and SSA Consulting Inc. is not responsible for those practices. Please review the relevant privacy policy when visiting another website.

10. Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose whether to submit an inquiry and what optional information to include. If you have questions about information you previously submitted, or would like to request access, correction or deletion, contact info@ssaconsultantinc.com.

We will consider your request in light of applicable obligations and any records we reasonably need to retain. We may need to confirm your identity before responding to a request involving personal information.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the website or our practices change. The latest version will appear on this page with an updated date.
